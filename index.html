Reading Nest Lending

Membership gives you access to the collection; each book has a simple lending fee based on its printed/reference MRP.

Book Lending Fee

10% of MRP
Per book · 14 days
  • Minimum lending fee: ₹20
  • Example: ₹499 MRP → ₹50
  • Example: ₹599 MRP → ₹60
  • Shown on every book card

Security Deposit

₹400
One-time · refundable
  • Applies to membership borrowing
  • Refundable when membership borrowing ends
  • Held as a book-security deposit
  • Separate from lending fees
How it works: Membership ₹199 + refundable ₹400 deposit + the lending fee for each book borrowed. Lending fee = 10% of the book's reference MRP, with a minimum of ₹20, for 14 days.

Reading Nest Borrowing Offer

Initial offer: Members can borrow up to 3 books.

From the next borrowing cycle: Members can borrow up to 2 books.

Borrowing period: 14 days per book.

Security deposit: ₹400 refundable deposit.

Late fee: ₹10 per book per day will be charged if a book is returned after the 14-day borrowing period.
